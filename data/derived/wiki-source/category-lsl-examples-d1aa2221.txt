==Example Scripts==

Come to this page to see short examples of how to use parts of LSL.

Go to the [[Script Library]]  page to see more complex examples that show how to combine parts of LSL.

For documentation of a '''User Function''' you can use the [[Template:LSL_Function/docs|LSL Function template]] with parameter <code>mode=user</code> set. It's very important to set this parameter to avoid adding the User Function to the Index of Standard LSL Functions! You can get a prototype of the template for filling in the blanks [[Template:LSL_Function/docs|here]].

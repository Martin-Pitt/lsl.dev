{{LSL Header|ml=*}}{{LSLC|Link}}{{LSLC|}}

Functions related to breaking and creating links between prims. Changes are indicated with the [[CHANGED_LINK]] flag in the [[changed]] {{LSLGC|Events|event}}.

Note: You cannot break or create links while an avatar {{LSLGC|Sit|sits}} on the object(s) involved. You must [[llUnSit|unsit]] them first.

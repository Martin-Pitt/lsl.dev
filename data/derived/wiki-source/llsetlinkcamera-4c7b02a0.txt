|spec
|caveats=
* Setting this will not update the cameras of seated avatars, it will only effect avatars that subsequently sit down. The camera settings have to be prepared in advance.
* The offsets {{LSLP|at}} and {{LSLP|eye}} are [[Viewer_coordinate_frames#Local|locally relative]] to the prim, if you want it relative to the seated [[Viewer_coordinate_frames#Agent|avatar]] (which likely has a custom sit rotation and offset) or the [[Viewer_coordinate_frames#Region|region]], you must do the computation yourself.<br>
Camera position and focus set by this function is a '''Prim Property'''. '''It will survive the script''' and it will survive prim taking and prim rezzing

|name=OBJECT_HOVER_HEIGHT

|desc=This is a flag used with [[llGetObjectDetails]] to get the [[Hover Height|hover height]] of the avatar.

If '''id''' is not an avatar, 0.0 is returned. Normal values are in the range {{Interval|gte=-2.0|lte=2.0|center=hover_height}} with a default of 0.0.

This value does ''not'' reflect the avatar shape's "Hover" slider, only the dynamic viewer setting (right-click on your avatar and pick "Hover Height").

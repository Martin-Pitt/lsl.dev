Tuesday, August 9, 2022

# Release Notes

This update adds Premium Plus support to llGetObjectDetails() in LSL. The OBJECT_ACCOUNT_LEVEL flag now returns 3 different values for present agents, depending on their account type: 0 = Base, 1 = Premium, 10 = Premium Plus.

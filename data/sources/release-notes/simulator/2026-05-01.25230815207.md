- The Second Life 26.2 “Flat UI” Beta viewer has even more fixes, plus support for moderating parcel & region voice chat: [https://releasenotes.secondlife.com/viewer/26.2.0.25021396775.html](https://releasenotes.secondlife.com/viewer/26.2.0.25021396775.html)
- For Firestorm users, we recommend running at least the Firestorm 7.2.4 Beta viewer at a minimum, which should be up to date with voice changes found in the Second Life 26.1 viewer.

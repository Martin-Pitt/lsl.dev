# Release Notes

### Maintenance Grappa + Happy hour

We have merged both active maintenance branches into one.

### NEW

An update to Maintenance Grappa + Happy Hour to fix media playback bug (BUG-231356).

#### Quality of life fixes

NOTE: We have restored prior Toggle to Talk behavior. While Push to Talk is still on the roadmap, we want to make it very easy for residents to select their preferred Speak button behavior.

Did you ever want to disable the default collision sound? Now you can!

You’re unique, so is your computer, we’ve tried to recognize that more and not forget your credentials after updates to your OS or SecondLife.

Did you ever sit somewhere unexpected? We have too… Fixed!

Improved Speak button state accuracy under certain conditions.

Bulk download of textures and pics! and more… :)

#### UI and rendering updates

Updated favicons to match our new logo.

More water reflections with ALM on!

A handful of EEP fixes.

A change to build unit increments which we hope will make builders happy.

#### Crash fixes

We like to think Sisyphus would respect our efforts.

#### Grappa

[Grappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grappa) is an alcoholic beverage: a fragrant, grape-based pomace brandy of Italian origin that contains 35 to 60 percent alcohol by volume (70 to 120 US proof). Grappa is traditionally produced in Northern Italy and is also widely consumed in places like Argentina, Bulgaria, Uruguay and Galicia (better known as Spanish ourujo).

#### Happy hour

[Happy hour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_hour) is a marketing term for a time when a venue (such as a restaurant, bar, bowling alley, stadium, state fair, or county fair) offers discounts on alcoholic drinks. Free appetizers and discounted menu items are often served during happy hour.

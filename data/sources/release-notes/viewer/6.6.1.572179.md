# Release Notes

### Maintenance Nomayo

#### UI improvements

Check out the minimap (Ctrl+Shift+M) and its fun new options; North at Top or Camera at Top, Parcel lines? You decide now.

#### Usability

- Landmarks should now work more like other assets for cut/copy actions.
- Animation uploads have joint name fixes
- Improved media playback responsiveness
- Crash fixes.

#### Nomayo

The Nomayo is a peach colored cocktail made from gin, St-Germain elderflower liqueur, Aperol, lemon juice and champagne, and served in a chilled cocktail glass.

# Release Notes

Welcome to emojis 😀! This viewer is based on a contribution by Kitty Barnett, and adds the ability to insert and display emojis in text chat. Emojis are encoded as their corresponding unicode characters, and can also be added using the system emoji tools on Windows (Win+.) and Mac (Ctrl-Cmd-space).

Many thanks to Kitty for the initial submission and much followup and revision.

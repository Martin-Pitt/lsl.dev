# Release Notes

### Maint. Preferences, Positions and Paste fixes

Searching Preferences will now include all the text in Notification Alerts.

Sounds will now be heard based on the users choice of avatar or camera position.

Paste of No Copy objects will be available in context menus, but No Copy enforcement remains intact. Conditional Copy/Paste error in rigged mesh overhaul resolved.

Plus, as you may have guessed, more crash fixes!

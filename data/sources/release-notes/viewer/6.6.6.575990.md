# Release Notes

### Maintenance Nomayo Hotfix

This release was an urgent fix for transparency “alpha” blending issues. In cases of many layers of textures that included transparencies, this would cause some of the lower layers to not render at all.

We didn’t want to ruin any Halloween costumes!

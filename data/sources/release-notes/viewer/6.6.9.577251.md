# Release Notes

### Performance Floater & Auto FPS

The new performance floater can be found under the World Menu, Improve Graphics Speed. Here you can:

- See your FPS (Frames per Second)
- Adjust your graphics settings
- See the complexity of the avatars around you and set the Max Complexity.
- See your avatar’s complexity with your attachments. Here you can see a ranking of each attachment and how it may effect your performance. You can even use this menu to detach attachments.
- Your active Huds.

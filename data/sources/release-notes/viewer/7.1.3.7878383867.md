# Release Notes

### Emoji

Welcome to emojis 👋 😀! This viewer is based on a contribution by Kitty Barnett, and adds the ability to insert and display emojis in text chat and other useful places. Emojis are encoded as their corresponding unicode characters and may be accessed from the chat interface.

Many thanks to Kitty 🐈 for the initial submission and much followup and revision.

Please remember to file any new Feature Requests or Bug Reports at [https://feedback.secondlife.com](https://feedback.secondlife.com)

#### Known Issues

MacOS system emoji picker is not functioning as expected and will be fixed in a future release.

# Release Notes

### Maintenance Xenial eXpansion

Xenial - Hospitable, especially to visiting strangers or foreigners

X here marks the spot for many viewer usability improvements including:

- Saving you some clicks by allowing the deletion of folders containing worn items. No need to first detach those unwanted demo’s.
- In case you only wish to detach, Alt+Shift+R will now detach selected attachments so you can remove those less fashionable items at rapid speed.
- The time format has been changed to show the more superior SLT instead of PDT.
- The group join fee will be shown when leaving a group so you can be extra sure you want to leave it.
- Shift+Alt+N will now open the Notifications floater for those of you that may have forgotten how to open it.
- Saving you more clicks as group notices can now be accessed directly from the People floaters Groups tab. Hover that mouse cursor over the group name for access.
- Said group notices will now default as being sorted by date with the newest first \o/

See more along with plenty of bug fixes below.

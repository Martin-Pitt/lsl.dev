# Release Notes

### Maint C: Cthulodon

Cthulodon[,](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/File:Cthulodon.webp) a completely made up fictional beast that has the tentacles characteristic of Cthulu as well as dinosaurian traits which together create something truly terrifying. Fortunately, such a creature would likely only be found in the world of Second Life and could possibly be the result of an avatar requiring their skeleton to be reset.

Previously, the magic to reset your avatar only worked locally but with this release, resetting your avatars skeleton will now reset it for others too!

Find more about other included fixes listed below.

#### Previous release notes

Maintenance A: Atlasaurus

This release marks the beginning of a new alphabet of Maintenance releases which will be themed after Dinosaurs!

Starting with the almighty Atlasaurus (not to be confused with the almighty [A](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/File:Atlasaurus.webp)tlas Linden). This titan herbivore is predicted to be 20m long and weighing 15 tons. It was discovered in the Atlas Mountains of Morocco where the titan Atlasaurus was said to hold up the heavens.

This Atlasaurus release brings new options to help with taking multiple objects so you can choose whether to take them all into your inventory as one or separately as individual objects. Better MOAP URL handling has also been implemented here in order for users to not only be able to CTRL+C or Cmd+C copy but also to see decoded URL payloads for easy inspection.

Adding to this are some smaller fixes which are listed below.

An attachment is an object (clothing, weapons, jewelry, shoes, etc) that can be attached to an [[avatar]] at a specific location. Usually location is set by the creator of the object. The default location otherwise is the [[ATTACH_RHAND|right hand]].

Attachments are linked to bones. When a bone is animated, all attachment locations linked to that bone will move identically to the bone they are linked to.

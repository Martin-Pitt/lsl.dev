### Annotated signature, as in the interpolation guide

`InterpolateFloatHermite` returns a point on a Hermite curve through `b` and `c`, steered by the neighbouring points `a` and `d`.

<FunctionSignature returns="float" name="InterpolateFloatHermite">
<Param type="float" name="a">The point before the segment. Only its slope matters.</Param>
<Param type="float" name="b">Start of the segment, returned when `t` is `0.0`.</Param>
<Param type="float" name="c">End of the segment, returned when `t` is `1.0`.</Param>
<Param type="float" name="d">The point after the segment.</Param>
<Param type="float" name="t">Position along the segment, from `0.0` to `1.0`.</Param>
<Param type="float" name="tens">Tension. `1.0` pulls the tangents to zero and gives sharp corners; `-1.0` makes the curve loose. Pass `0.0` for a Catmull-Rom curve.</Param>
<Param type="float" name="bias">Bias. Positive values lean the curve towards `a`, negative values towards `d`. Pass `0.0` for none.</Param>
<Returns>The interpolated value. Pass the result to [`llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast`](/functions/llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast/) or use it in a timer loop.</Returns>
</FunctionSignature>

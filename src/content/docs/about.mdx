## Sources

The site merges two sources and states on each page which one a piece of content comes from.

| Content | Source |
|---|---|
| Signatures, parameter names and descriptions, return types, constant values and groups, categories, delays, energy and other metadata | [LSL Definitions](https://github.com/secondlife/lsl-definitions) (`lsl_definitions.yaml`) |
| Descriptive prose, caveats, notes and examples | The official [LSL Wiki](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/LSL_Portal) on the Second Life Wiki |

Where the two disagree on structured facts, LSL Definitions wins. Wiki prose is kept as written in the original revisions; it is not summarized or rewritten. The main reference leaves out functions, events and constants that LSL Definitions marks as deprecated or private. Historical wiki pages remain in the [wiki archive](/wiki/).

## How the site is built

Both snapshots are checked into the repository. LSL Definitions is refreshed from GitHub by an automated sync; the wiki snapshot is an offline export. An offline importer turns them into the catalog used by the site, and neither the importer nor the build makes requests to the Second Life Wiki. Wiki markup is only partly rendered: templates the renderer does not know are shown as their original markup rather than guessed at, and the original wikitext of every page can be downloaded from its archive page.

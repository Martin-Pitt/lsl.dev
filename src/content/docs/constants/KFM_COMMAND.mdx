Command used in the options parameter of [llSetKeyframedMotion](/functions/llSetKeyframedMotion/) .followed by one of: [KFM_CMD_STOP](/constants/KFM_CMD_STOP/), [KFM_CMD_PLAY](/constants/KFM_CMD_PLAY/), [KFM_CMD_PAUSE](/constants/KFM_CMD_PAUSE/) to play , stop or pause the motion .

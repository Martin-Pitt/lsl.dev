<LSLConstant name="KFM_MODE" />

used in [llSetKeyframedMotion](/functions/llSetKeyframedMotion/) . followed by one of: [KFM_LOOP](/constants/KFM_LOOP/), [KFM_REVERSE](/constants/KFM_REVERSE/), [KFM_FORWARD](/constants/KFM_FORWARD/), or [KFM_PING_PONG](/constants/KFM_PING_PONG/) will specify the playback mode. Defaults to [KFM_FORWARD](/constants/KFM_FORWARD/). Must be specified when the keyframe list is provided.

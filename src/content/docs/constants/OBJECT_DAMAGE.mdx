<LSLConstant name="OBJECT_DAMAGE" description />

Gets the damage amount delivered by a prim on collision.

## Caveats

- Collideable damage as set via [llSetDamage](/functions/llSetDamage/), [PRIM_DAMAGE](/constants/PRIM_DAMAGE/), and [REZ_DAMAGE](/constants/REZ_DAMAGE/) is limited to 100 maximum

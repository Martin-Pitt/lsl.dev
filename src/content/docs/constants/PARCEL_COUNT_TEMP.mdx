<LSLConstant name="PARCEL_COUNT_TEMP" description />

Used with [llGetParcelPrimCount](/functions/llGetParcelPrimCount/) to get the total land impact of [temporary](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/Category:LSL_Temporary) objects.

## Caveats

- Meshes are different than ordinary Prims and Sculpts when set Temporary.

  They still count against the Parcel and Sim Prim Limits. See: [Mesh_Prim_Equivalence_test](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/Mesh_Prim_Equivalence_test#temporary-meshes)

Used to get or set the full-bright setting of a prim's face.

## Usage

### \[ [PRIM_FULLBRIGHT](/constants/PRIM_FULLBRIGHT/), [integer](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/integer) **face**, [integer](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/integer) **boolean** \]

- [integer](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/integer) **face** — a [face](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/Face) number or [ALL_SIDES](/constants/ALL_SIDES/)
- [integer](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/integer) **boolean**

When used with [llSetPrimitiveParams](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/LlSetPrimitiveParams) & [llSetLinkPrimitiveParams](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/LlSetPrimitiveParams#llSetLinkPrimitiveParams)

### [llGetPrimitiveParams](/functions/llGetPrimitiveParams/)(\[ [PRIM_FULLBRIGHT](/constants/PRIM_FULLBRIGHT/), [integer](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/integer) **face** \]);

- [integer](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/integer) **face** — a [face](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/Face) number or [ALL_SIDES](/constants/ALL_SIDES/)
- [integer](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/integer) **boolean**

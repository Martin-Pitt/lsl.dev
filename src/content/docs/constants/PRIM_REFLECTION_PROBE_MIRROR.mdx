<LSLConstant name="PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_MIRROR" />

Used with [PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE/). When enabled, objects with low-roughness PBR materials objects act as a mirror. Note that mirrors do not reflect avatars unless [PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_DYNAMIC](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_DYNAMIC/) is also set. Rendering mirrors has a performance cost.

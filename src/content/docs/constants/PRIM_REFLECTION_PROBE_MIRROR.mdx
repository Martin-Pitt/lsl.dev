Used with [PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE/). When enabled, objects with low-roughness PBR materials objects act as a mirror. The probe must also be a box probe ([PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_BOX](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_BOX/));[^v1] the mirror plane is the probe's local +Z face,[^v2] and the viewer only renders the mirror probe nearest to the camera, so only one mirror is active at a time.[^v3] Note that mirrors do not reflect avatars unless [PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_DYNAMIC](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_DYNAMIC/) is also set. Rendering mirrors has a performance cost.

`PRIM_SCULPT_FLAG_MIRROR` will cause a mirror of the sculpted prim to rendered. The sculpted prim is mirrored over the X axis.

[Sculpted Prims: FAQ](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/Sculpted_Prims:_FAQ)

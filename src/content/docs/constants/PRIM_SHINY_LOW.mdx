Sets the lowest intensity legacy shininess.

## Notes

- The appearance is equivalent to [PRIM_SPECULAR](/constants/PRIM_SPECULAR/) with `texture`=[TEXTURE_BLANK](/constants/TEXTURE_BLANK/), `glossiness`=0 and `environment`=64.

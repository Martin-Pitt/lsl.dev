<LSLConstant name="PRIM_TEXGEN_PLANAR" />

Used with [PRIM_TEXGEN](/constants/PRIM_TEXGEN/) to set the texture mapping mode, specifically that the texture repeats units are in texture repeats per half meter. This is in contrast to the in-world editing tool, in which the planar texture scaling units are repeats per meter.

<LSLConstant name="TOUCH_INVALID_FACE" />

Returned by [llDetectedTouchFace](/functions/llDetectedTouchFace/) when the [touch position](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/LSL_Touch_Position) is not valid.

Specifically when...

- The avatar's viewer does not support face touch detection.
- The touch has moved off the surface of the prim.
- The triggering event is not a touch event.

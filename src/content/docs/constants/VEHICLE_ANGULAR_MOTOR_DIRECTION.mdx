vector of angular velocity (in radian/second) that the vehicle will try to achieve

## Caveats

- [VEHICLE_ANGULAR_MOTOR_DIRECTION](/constants/VEHICLE_ANGULAR_MOTOR_DIRECTION/) magnitude is capped at 4 \* [PI](/constants/PI/) (2 revolutions per second total)

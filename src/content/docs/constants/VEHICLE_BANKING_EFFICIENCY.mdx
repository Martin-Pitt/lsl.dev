<LSLConstant name="VEHICLE_BANKING_EFFICIENCY" description />

slider between -1.00 (leans out of turns), 0 (no banking) and +1.00 (leans into turns). This parameter makes banking affect steering, not the other way around. Use vehicle [angular motors](/constants/VEHICLE_ANGULAR_MOTOR_DIRECTION/) to bank the vehicle.

## Known issues

From the issue templates included by the wiki article:

- SVC-5205 (bug): Negative values for VEHICLE_BANKING_EFFICIENCY behave the same as positive values.

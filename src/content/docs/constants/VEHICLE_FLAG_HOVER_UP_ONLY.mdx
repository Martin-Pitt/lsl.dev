## Caveats

- When used with non-zero [VEHICLE_HOVER_HEIGHT](/constants/VEHICLE_HOVER_HEIGHT/) and [VEHICLE_BUOYANCY](/constants/VEHICLE_BUOYANCY/) then the buoyancy effect will be disabled when the vehicle's (root prim) position is higher than its hover height.
  - This has been confirmed to be a misfeature[^wiki-55b893].

[^wiki-55b893]: See [User_talk:Andrew_Linden](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/User_talk:Andrew_Linden#vehicle-flag-hover-up-only) for details.

## Known issues

- [SVC-1972](https://jira.secondlife.com/browse/SVC-1972): `VEHICLE_FLAG_HOVER_UP_ONLY` doesn't seem to do anything

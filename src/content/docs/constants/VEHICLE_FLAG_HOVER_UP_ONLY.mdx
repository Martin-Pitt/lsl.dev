## Caveats

- When used with non-zero [VEHICLE_HOVER_HEIGHT](/constants/VEHICLE_HOVER_HEIGHT/) and [VEHICLE_BUOYANCY](/constants/VEHICLE_BUOYANCY/) then the buoyancy effect will be disabled when the vehicle's (root prim) position is higher than its hover height.
- This has been confirmed to be a `misfeature``{{Footnote|See [[User_talk:Andrew_Linden#VEHICLE_FLAG_HOVER_UP_ONLY]] for details.|See User_talk:Andrew_Linden#VEHICLE_FLAG_HOVER_UP_ONLY for details.}}`.

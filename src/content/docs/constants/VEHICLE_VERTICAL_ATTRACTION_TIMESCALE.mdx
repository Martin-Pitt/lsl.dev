<LSLConstant name="VEHICLE_VERTICAL_ATTRACTION_TIMESCALE" description />

exponential timescale (in seconds) for the vehicle to align its z-axis to the world z-axis

## Notes

- setting [VEHICLE_VERTICAL_ATTRACTION_TIMESCALE](/constants/VEHICLE_VERTICAL_ATTRACTION_TIMESCALE/) greater than 300.0 disables it.

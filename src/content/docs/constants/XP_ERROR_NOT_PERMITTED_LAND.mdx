Returned when an experience permission request ([llRequestExperiencePermissions](/functions/llRequestExperiencePermissions/)) is made where the experience is blocked or not enabled for the land. [Key-value store](/features/experience/#key-value-store-vs-permissions) calls do not depend on the land and do not return it[^xp-land]

[^xp-land]: Confirmed in-world by Nexii Malthus (October 2026); not stated on the wiki or in the LSL definitions

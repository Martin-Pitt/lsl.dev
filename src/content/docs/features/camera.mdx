Each avatar has one camera, controlled by the viewer. Scripts can suggest a default view when someone sits, take over the camera with permission, or read where the camera is.

Scripts never own the camera. They ask, and the viewer applies it. Two permissions are involved: [`PERMISSION_CONTROL_CAMERA`](/constants/PERMISSION_CONTROL_CAMERA/) to set camera parameters and [`PERMISSION_TRACK_CAMERA`](/constants/PERMISSION_TRACK_CAMERA/) to read the camera position and rotation.

`PERMISSION_CONTROL_CAMERA` is automatically granted for attached or sat-on objects, so HUDs, seats and vehicles get it without a prompt.

<Columns>
<Column>
<Box title="Concepts">

- A [sit camera](#sit-camera) needs no permission
  - Set an [eye offset](/functions/llSetCameraEyeOffset/) and a [focus offset](/functions/llSetCameraAtOffset/) on the prim; the viewer uses them when an avatar [sits there](/functions/llSitTarget/)
  - [`llSetLinkCamera`](/functions/llSetLinkCamera/) does the same for any prim in the linkset
- A [scripted camera](/functions/llSetCameraParams/) takes over the view
  - Parameters set the camera position, focus, distance, lag and thresholds, and can lock either point in place
  - They stay in effect until [cleared](/functions/llClearCameraParams/)
  - Vehicles, follow cameras and cutscenes are built this way
  - A script can also [force mouselook](/functions/llForceMouselook/)
- [Reading the camera](/functions/llGetCameraPos/) needs `PERMISSION_TRACK_CAMERA`
  - [Position](/functions/llGetCameraPos/), [rotation](/functions/llGetCameraRot/), [field of view](/functions/llGetCameraFOV/) and [aspect ratio](/functions/llGetCameraAspect/)
  - For example to aim along the view or [place a HUD element](/functions/llWorldPosToHUD/) in the world
- Permissions are [requested](/functions/llRequestPermissions/) and arrive in [`run_time_permissions`](/events/run_time_permissions/)
- Camera scripts are usually paired with [control capture](/features/input/), so the user can steer while the script drives the view

<Box title="Additional">

- [`llRequestPermissions`](/functions/llRequestPermissions/) — the two camera permissions
  - [`PERMISSION_CONTROL_CAMERA`](/constants/PERMISSION_CONTROL_CAMERA/) — control the agent's camera; the agent must be sitting on or attached to the object, and it is revoked on standing up or detaching
  - [`PERMISSION_TRACK_CAMERA`](/constants/PERMISSION_TRACK_CAMERA/) — track the agent's camera position and rotation
- [`llSetCameraParams`](/functions/llSetCameraParams/) — rules list of camera parameters
  - [`CAMERA_ACTIVE`](/constants/CAMERA_ACTIVE/) — turns scripted control of the camera on or off
  - [`CAMERA_POSITION`](/constants/CAMERA_POSITION/) and [`CAMERA_FOCUS`](/constants/CAMERA_FOCUS/) — camera position and focus in region coordinates
  - [`CAMERA_POSITION_LOCKED`](/constants/CAMERA_POSITION_LOCKED/) and [`CAMERA_FOCUS_LOCKED`](/constants/CAMERA_FOCUS_LOCKED/) — lock either point so it does not move
  - [`CAMERA_DISTANCE`](/constants/CAMERA_DISTANCE/) — how far away the camera wants to be from its target
  - [`CAMERA_PITCH`](/constants/CAMERA_PITCH/) — aim straight ahead versus straight down, at the same distance
  - [`CAMERA_FOCUS_OFFSET`](/constants/CAMERA_FOCUS_OFFSET/) — focus position relative to the target
  - [`CAMERA_BEHINDNESS_ANGLE`](/constants/CAMERA_BEHINDNESS_ANGLE/) and [`CAMERA_BEHINDNESS_LAG`](/constants/CAMERA_BEHINDNESS_LAG/) — how the camera follows changes in target rotation
  - [`CAMERA_POSITION_LAG`](/constants/CAMERA_POSITION_LAG/) and [`CAMERA_FOCUS_LAG`](/constants/CAMERA_FOCUS_LAG/) — how much the camera lags as it moves and aims
  - [`CAMERA_POSITION_THRESHOLD`](/constants/CAMERA_POSITION_THRESHOLD/) and [`CAMERA_FOCUS_THRESHOLD`](/constants/CAMERA_FOCUS_THRESHOLD/) — spheres within which target motion is ignored

</Box>

Environments, from the Environmental Enhancement Project (EEP), define the sky, water and day cycle. Regions and parcels have an environment, and each avatar can see an override on top of it.

Scripts can read the sun and moon positions, change a parcel's or region's environment where the owner allows it, and give a single avatar a temporary environment through an [experience](/features/experience/). Wind, water height and ground shape are separate region properties, not part of the environment.

<Columns>
<Column>
<Box title="Concepts">

- Reading the environment
  - Sun and moon [direction](/functions/llGetSunDirection/) and [rotation](/functions/llGetMoonRotation/) for the parcel, falling back to the region's; or [for the region](/functions/llGetRegionSunDirection/) itself
  - [Day length](/functions/llGetDayLength/), [day offset](/functions/llGetDayOffset/) and [time of day](/functions/llGetTimeOfDay/), each also [region-wide](/functions/llGetRegionTimeOfDay/)
  - [Individual environment settings at a position](/functions/llGetEnvironment/)
- Parcel and region environment
  - [Swap in a whole environment asset](/functions/llReplaceEnvironment/)
  - [Change individual settings](/functions/llSetEnvironment/)
  - Both need the land rights to change the environment
- Per-avatar environment
  - Inside an experience, a script can [override what one avatar sees](/functions/llReplaceAgentEnvironment/), or [set individual settings for them](/functions/llSetAgentEnvironment/), with a transition time
  - The override is temporary
- Weather and ground
  - [Wind](/functions/llWind/), [water height](/functions/llWater/) and [the ground shape](/functions/llGround/) are region properties scripts can read

<Box title="Additional">

- [`llGetEnvironment`](/functions/llGetEnvironment/) — read-only and settable parameters
  - [`ENVIRONMENT_DAYINFO`](/constants/ENVIRONMENT_DAYINFO/) — day length, day offset and seconds since midnight
  - [`SKY_SUN`](/constants/SKY_SUN/) — sun rotation, scale, color, direction and lighting
  - [`WATER_FOG`](/constants/WATER_FOG/) — underwater fog color, density and modulation
- [`llSetEnvironment`](/functions/llSetEnvironment/), [`llReplaceEnvironment`](/functions/llReplaceEnvironment/) and the per-avatar functions — return an error code
  - [`ENV_OK`](/constants/ENV_OK/) — the change will be attempted
  - [`ENV_NO_PERMISSIONS`](/constants/ENV_NO_PERMISSIONS/) — the script lacks permissions to modify the environment here
  - [`ENV_NOT_EXPERIENCE`](/constants/ENV_NOT_EXPERIENCE/) — attempted to change environments outside an experience
- Ground and weather
  - [`llGround`](/functions/llGround/) — ground height below the prim
  - [`llGroundNormal`](/functions/llGroundNormal/) — ground surface normal
  - [`llGroundSlope`](/functions/llGroundSlope/) — ground slope
  - [`llGroundContour`](/functions/llGroundContour/) — direction with no change in elevation
  - [`llWater`](/functions/llWater/) — water height
  - [`llWind`](/functions/llWind/) — wind velocity

</Box>

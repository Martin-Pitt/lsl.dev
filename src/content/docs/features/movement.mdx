There are three ways to move an object. Non-physical movement sets the position and rotation directly. Keyframed motion plays a smooth path computed by the region. Physical movement applies forces and lets the [physics](/features/physics/) engine move the object.

Non-physical and keyframed movement are predictable and suit doors, platforms and rides. Physical movement suits vehicles, balls and projectiles.

<Columns>
<Column>
<Box title="Concepts">

- Non-physical movement sets the [position](/functions/llSetPos/) and [rotation](/functions/llSetRot/) in one step
  - [`llSetRegionPos`](/functions/llSetRegionPos/) moves up to the region's size in one call; [`llSetPos`](/functions/llSetPos/) is limited per call (up to 10m)
  - Child prims use [local coordinates](/functions/llSetLocalRot/)
- [Keyframed motion](/functions/llSetKeyframedMotion/): give a list of position and rotation offsets with times, and the region moves the object smoothly, looping or ping-ponging if asked
  - Works on non-physical objects and costs little script time
- Physical objects move by [impulses](/functions/llApplyImpulse/), [forces](/functions/llSetForce/), velocities and targets
  - [Move-to](/functions/llMoveToTarget/) and [look-at](/functions/llLookAt/) targets act like springs
  - [`llSetVelocity`](/functions/llSetVelocity/) and [`llSetAngularVelocity`](/functions/llSetAngularVelocity/) set motion directly
- Position and rotation [targets](/functions/llTarget/) fire [events](/events/at_target/) when the object arrives or leaves, without polling
  - [`moving_start`](/events/moving_start/) and [`moving_end`](/events/moving_end/) report when the object starts and stops moving
- [`llTargetOmega`](/functions/llTargetOmega/) spins an object
  - On non-physical objects the spin is drawn by the viewer only, so it costs nothing on the region
- The [vehicle](/functions/llSetVehicleType/) engine adds tuned motors, friction and stabilisers on top of physics
  - Choose a vehicle type, then adjust [parameters](/functions/llSetVehicleFloatParam/) and [flags](/functions/llSetVehicleFlags/)
  - Vehicles are steered with [captured controls](/features/input/)

</Box>
</Column>

<Box title="Additional">

- [`llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast`](/functions/llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast/) — move or spin any prim by link number
  - [`PRIM_POSITION`](/constants/PRIM_POSITION/) and [`PRIM_ROTATION`](/constants/PRIM_ROTATION/) — position and rotation
  - [`PRIM_POS_LOCAL`](/constants/PRIM_POS_LOCAL/) and [`PRIM_ROT_LOCAL`](/constants/PRIM_ROT_LOCAL/) — local position and rotation of a prim
  - [`PRIM_OMEGA`](/constants/PRIM_OMEGA/) — client-side rotation, analogous to `llTargetOmega`
- [`llSetKeyframedMotion`](/functions/llSetKeyframedMotion/) — [options](/constants/groups/KeyframedMotionParam/), [modes](/constants/groups/KeyframedMotionMode/), [commands](/constants/groups/KeyframedMotionCommand/) and [data flags](/constants/groups/KeyframedMotionData/)
  - [`KFM_MODE`](/constants/KFM_MODE/) — playback mode, defaults to `KFM_FORWARD`
  - [`KFM_COMMAND`](/constants/KFM_COMMAND/) — play, stop, or pause the motion
  - [`KFM_DATA`](/constants/KFM_DATA/) — whether keyframes hold translation, rotation or both
- [`llSetVehicleType`](/functions/llSetVehicleType/) — [vehicle types](/constants/groups/VehicleType/), then [float](/constants/groups/VehicleFloatParam/), [vector](/constants/groups/VehicleVectorParam/) and [rotation](/constants/groups/VehicleRotationParam/) parameters and [flags](/constants/groups/VehicleFlag/)

A prim plays sounds from its inventory or by asset key. Attached sounds follow the prim, and a prim has one attached sound at a time. Triggered sounds play at a fixed position and can overlap.

Volume runs from 0.0 to 1.0. Sound radius, queueing and preloading help sounds start on time and stay in range.

Streaming music is not a prim sound: it belongs to the [parcel](#parcel-music).

<Columns>
<Column>
<Box title="Concepts">

- Attached sounds: [play once](/functions/llPlaySound/), [loop](/functions/llLoopSound/) or [stop](/functions/llStopSound/)
  - Only one sound is attached to a prim at a time; a new one replaces it
  - The Link variants [play](/functions/llLinkPlaySound/) and [stop](/functions/llLinkStopSound/) on any prim in the linkset
- Triggered sounds [play at the prim's position](/functions/llTriggerSound/) without being attached to it, optionally [limited to a box](/functions/llTriggerSoundLimited/)
  - They do not move with the object and cannot be stopped or adjusted
- [Adjust the volume](/functions/llAdjustSoundVolume/) of a playing sound, [limit how far it carries](/functions/llSetSoundRadius/), and [preload sounds](/functions/llPreloadSound/) so they play without delay
- With [queueing](/functions/llSetSoundQueueing/) on, the next sound waits for the current one to finish
  - Engines and weapons play a start sound, a loop for as long as needed, and an end sound without a pop at the transitions
- A [master loop](/functions/llLoopSoundMaster/) and [slave sounds](/functions/llLoopSoundSlave/) keep several prims in time with each other
- [Replace the default collision sound](/functions/llCollisionSound/); rezzed projectiles get their sounds [at rez time](/functions/llRezObjectWithParams/)

Call `llSetSoundQueueing(TRUE)`, then queue a start sound, a looped sound and an end sound; each waits for the one before it. The queue is one level deep.

Streaming music belongs to the parcel, not the prim. The object owner must match the landowner or land group.

<Box title="Additional">

- [`llLinkPlaySound`](/functions/llLinkPlaySound/) — playback flags ([`PlaySoundFlag`](/constants/groups/PlaySoundFlag/))
  - [`SOUND_PLAY`](/constants/SOUND_PLAY/) — Play once, attached (default)
  - [`SOUND_LOOP`](/constants/SOUND_LOOP/) — Loop until stopped
  - [`SOUND_TRIGGER`](/constants/SOUND_TRIGGER/) — Trigger once at the prim's position, not attached; overrides the other flags
  - [`SOUND_SYNC`](/constants/SOUND_SYNC/) — Synchronise to the nearest [sound master](/functions/llLoopSoundMaster/)
- [`llRezObjectWithParams`](/functions/llRezObjectWithParams/) — set a projectile's sounds before the object exists
  - [`REZ_SOUND`](/constants/REZ_SOUND/) — Attach and play a sound, optionally looping
  - [`REZ_SOUND_COLLIDE`](/constants/REZ_SOUND_COLLIDE/) — Sound to play on collision with an object, the ground or an avatar
- [`llCollisionSound`](/functions/llCollisionSound/) — an empty string only suppresses collision sounds ([Physics](/features/physics/))
- [`llSetParcelMusicURL`](/functions/llSetParcelMusicURL/) and [`llGetParcelMusicURL`](/functions/llGetParcelMusicURL/) — the parcel's streaming music URL ([Land](/features/land/))

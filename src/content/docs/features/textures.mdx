title: "Textures & Materials"

Each face of a prim has its own surface. The original (Blinn-Phong) system combines a diffuse texture, color, alpha, normal and specular maps. PBR materials, based on glTF, use base color, normal, metallic-roughness and emissive maps, and can be overridden per face.

The two systems are set separately. Blinn-Phong texture repeats, offsets and rotation are set with [`PRIM_TEXTURE`](/constants/PRIM_TEXTURE/), [`PRIM_NORMAL`](/constants/PRIM_NORMAL/) and [`PRIM_SPECULAR`](/constants/PRIM_SPECULAR/); PBR transforms are set with the `PRIM_GLTF_*` rules or [glTF overrides](/functions/llSetLinkGLTFOverrides/).

Shape, light, glow and other prim properties live under [Prims](/features/prims/); texture animation under [Effects](/features/effects/).

<Columns>
<Column>
<Box title="Concepts">

- Textures: [set one by inventory name or key](/functions/llSetTexture/), [on any prim](/functions/llSetLinkTexture/), and [read it back](/functions/llGetTexture/)
  - Adjust [repeats](/functions/llScaleTexture/), [offset](/functions/llOffsetTexture/) and [rotation](/functions/llRotateTexture/); the [mapping mode](/constants/PRIM_TEXGEN/) sets how repeats are measured
- Colors are vectors from 0.0 to 1.0, [set per face](/functions/llSetColor/)
  - [Alpha](/functions/llSetAlpha/) runs from 0.0 (invisible) to 1.0
  - The [alpha mode](/constants/PRIM_ALPHA_MODE/) sets how transparent textures are drawn
- Blinn-Phong materials: [normal](/constants/PRIM_NORMAL/) and [specular](/constants/PRIM_SPECULAR/) maps, [shininess and bump presets](/constants/PRIM_BUMP_SHINY/) add surface detail to the legacy system
  - [Full bright](/constants/PRIM_FULLBRIGHT/) and [glow](/constants/PRIM_GLOW/) change how a face is lit
- PBR materials: [apply a material asset](/functions/llSetRenderMaterial/) to faces, or [override parts of it](/functions/llSetLinkGLTFOverrides/) (base color, alpha, metallic, roughness, emissive) without replacing it
  - [Check whether a face uses PBR](/functions/llIsLinkGLTFMaterial/)
- [Reflection probes](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE/) control what PBR surfaces reflect nearby
- [Frame animation and scrolling](/functions/llSetTextureAnim/) are covered under [Effects](/features/effects/)
- A face can [show a web page](/functions/llSetPrimMediaParams/); media settings control the URL, size, controls and who may interact
- The [`changed`](/events/changed/) event reports [color](/constants/CHANGED_COLOR/), [material](/constants/CHANGED_RENDER_MATERIAL/) and [media](/constants/CHANGED_MEDIA/) changes

<Box title="Additional">

- [`llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast`](/functions/llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast/) — Blinn-Phong face rules
  - [`PRIM_TEXTURE`](/constants/PRIM_TEXTURE/) — Diffuse texture, repeats, offsets and rotation
  - [`PRIM_TEXGEN`](/constants/PRIM_TEXGEN/) — Mapping mode ([`PrimTexgen`](/constants/groups/PrimTexgen/)): [default](/constants/PRIM_TEXGEN_DEFAULT/) or [planar](/constants/PRIM_TEXGEN_PLANAR/)
  - [`PRIM_COLOR`](/constants/PRIM_COLOR/) — Color and alpha, from 0.0 (clear) to 1.0 (solid)
  - [`PRIM_ALPHA_MODE`](/constants/PRIM_ALPHA_MODE/) — [Blend](/constants/PRIM_ALPHA_MODE_BLEND/), [none](/constants/PRIM_ALPHA_MODE_NONE/), [mask](/constants/PRIM_ALPHA_MODE_MASK/) with a cutoff, or [emissive](/constants/PRIM_ALPHA_MODE_EMISSIVE/) ([`AlphaMode`](/constants/groups/AlphaMode/))
  - [`PRIM_NORMAL`](/constants/PRIM_NORMAL/) — Normal map with its own repeats, offsets and rotation
  - [`PRIM_SPECULAR`](/constants/PRIM_SPECULAR/) — Specular map plus color, glossiness and environment
  - [`PRIM_BUMP_SHINY`](/constants/PRIM_BUMP_SHINY/) — Shininess ([`PrimShiny`](/constants/groups/PrimShiny/)) and bump preset ([`PrimBump`](/constants/groups/PrimBump/))
  - [`PRIM_FULLBRIGHT`](/constants/PRIM_FULLBRIGHT/), [`PRIM_GLOW`](/constants/PRIM_GLOW/) — Full-bright rendering and glow intensity
- [`llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast`](/functions/llSetLinkPrimitiveParamsFast/) — PBR face rules
  - [`PRIM_RENDER_MATERIAL`](/constants/PRIM_RENDER_MATERIAL/) — The material asset on a face
  - [`PRIM_GLTF_BASE_COLOR`](/constants/PRIM_GLTF_BASE_COLOR/) — Base color, alpha mode ([`GLTFAlphaMode`](/constants/groups/GLTFAlphaMode/)), alpha cutoff and double-sided; colors in linear space
  - [`PRIM_GLTF_NORMAL`](/constants/PRIM_GLTF_NORMAL/) — Normal map
  - [`PRIM_GLTF_METALLIC_ROUGHNESS`](/constants/PRIM_GLTF_METALLIC_ROUGHNESS/) — Occlusion, metallic and roughness
  - [`PRIM_GLTF_EMISSIVE`](/constants/PRIM_GLTF_EMISSIVE/) — Emission tint in linear space
  - [`PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE`](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE/) — Make the object a reflection probe ([`ReflectionProbeType`](/constants/groups/ReflectionProbeType/)): [box](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_BOX/), [dynamic](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_DYNAMIC/) or [mirror](/constants/PRIM_REFLECTION_PROBE_MIRROR/)
- [`llSetLinkGLTFOverrides`](/functions/llSetLinkGLTFOverrides/) — individual overrides ([`GLTFOverride`](/constants/groups/GLTFOverride/)), such as [`OVERRIDE_GLTF_BASE_COLOR_FACTOR`](/constants/OVERRIDE_GLTF_BASE_COLOR_FACTOR/), [`OVERRIDE_GLTF_METALLIC_FACTOR`](/constants/OVERRIDE_GLTF_METALLIC_FACTOR/), [`OVERRIDE_GLTF_ROUGHNESS_FACTOR`](/constants/OVERRIDE_GLTF_ROUGHNESS_FACTOR/) and [`OVERRIDE_GLTF_EMISSIVE_FACTOR`](/constants/OVERRIDE_GLTF_EMISSIVE_FACTOR/)
- [`llSetPrimMediaParams`](/functions/llSetPrimMediaParams/) and [`llSetLinkMedia`](/functions/llSetLinkMedia/) — media settings ([`PrimMediaParam`](/constants/groups/PrimMediaParam/))
  - [`PRIM_MEDIA_CURRENT_URL`](/constants/PRIM_MEDIA_CURRENT_URL/), [`PRIM_MEDIA_HOME_URL`](/constants/PRIM_MEDIA_HOME_URL/) — Current and home URL
  - [`PRIM_MEDIA_AUTO_PLAY`](/constants/PRIM_MEDIA_AUTO_PLAY/) — Play when a resident can view it
  - [`PRIM_MEDIA_PERMS_INTERACT`](/constants/PRIM_MEDIA_PERMS_INTERACT/) — Who may interact ([`PrimMediaPerm`](/constants/groups/PrimMediaPerm/))
  - Navigation controls: [standard](/constants/PRIM_MEDIA_CONTROLS_STANDARD/) or [mini](/constants/PRIM_MEDIA_CONTROLS_MINI/) ([`PrimMediaControl`](/constants/groups/PrimMediaControl/))
- [`changed`](/events/changed/) — [`CHANGED_COLOR`](/constants/CHANGED_COLOR/), [`CHANGED_TEXTURE`](/constants/CHANGED_TEXTURE/), [`CHANGED_RENDER_MATERIAL`](/constants/CHANGED_RENDER_MATERIAL/), [`CHANGED_MEDIA`](/constants/CHANGED_MEDIA/)

</Box>

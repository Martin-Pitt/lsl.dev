<LSLFunction name="llSetSoundRadius" />

Establishes a hard cut-off **radius** for audibility for [llPlaySound](/functions/llPlaySound/), [llLoopSound](/functions/llLoopSound/) and [llLinkPlaySound](/functions/llLinkPlaySound/) in non-triggered mode. Sounds are not heard until an observer's ear position enters the radius, and immediately stop when leaving the radius.[^v1] The viewer measures the distance from the camera by default, or from the avatar if the *MediaSoundsEarLocation* setting is changed;[^v2] for attached objects the distance is measured from the wearer's position, not the prim.[^v3] A radius below 0.1 m is treated as off.[^v4] Volume fading is still determined by the distance from the ear position, not the radius.

```lsl title="How to use" frame="terminal"
llSetSoundRadius(0.0);
```

## Caveats

- This function is a prim property, thus will survive script resets.
- Has no effect on [llTriggerSound](/functions/llTriggerSound/) or [llLinkPlaySound](/functions/llLinkPlaySound/) in triggered mode. Sounds will play regardless of listener position.
- [llTriggerSoundLimited](/functions/llTriggerSoundLimited/) can be used instead for a similar effect but the function always depends on **avatar** position, regardless of the viewer's ear setting.
- When used with [llPlaySound](/functions/llPlaySound/) or [llLinkPlaySound](/functions/llLinkPlaySound/) in non-looped mode, the sound will be played from the beginning only once, when the observer's ear position enters the radius for the first time. This can happen significantly later than a one-off sound was actually played.
- With [llLoopSound](/functions/llLoopSound/) or [llLinkPlaySound](/functions/llLinkPlaySound/) in looped mode, the sound will be played from the beginning every time the observer's ear position enters the radius.

## Examples

<details open>
<summary>Enable sound cutoff at 4 meters.</summary>

```lsl
llSetSoundRadius(4); //Enable sound cutoff at 4 meters.
```

</details>

<details>
<summary>Disable sound cutoff.</summary>

```lsl
llSetSoundRadius(0); //Disable sound cutoff.
```

</details>

## Notes

Pass 0 to this function to disable it; the viewer treats any radius below 0.1 m as off.[^v5]

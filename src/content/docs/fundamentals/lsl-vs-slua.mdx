SLua is documented on the sister site [slua.dev](https://slua.dev/). Its guide [LSL vs SLua](https://slua.dev/fundamentals/lsl-vs-slua/) compares the two languages side by side for LSL scripters.

## In the viewer

The Second Life Viewer 26.5 script editor treats the two as different source languages. Its **Compiler** dropdown offers LSL on Mono, LSL Legacy, LSL on Lua and Lua; the two Lua entries are only enabled on regions whose simulator features report `LuaScriptsEnabled`.[^v1] On such regions a new script defaults to Lua, both from the Build tool's Content tab and in your inventory, so pick LSL explicitly when you want it.[^v2] Line numbers count from 0 in an LSL script and from 1 in a Lua script.[^v3] Details are under [Script editor](/fundamentals/script-editor/).

Guides are longer articles and tutorials that explain a topic and build up working code. For short
how-tos that do one task, see [Recipes](/recipes/); for the language itself, see
[Fundamentals](/fundamentals/).

- [Learn LSL](/guides/learn-lsl/): a path into Second Life scripting, from the first script to
  useful interactions.
- [Working with link numbers](/guides/link-numbers/): find prims by name in your own linkset or
  another object, and keep a pool of reusable prims in linkset data.
- [Texture coordinates](/guides/texture-coordinates/): turn pixel positions in a texture atlas or
  spritesheet into `PRIM_TEXTURE` repeats and offsets.
- [Constraining rotations](/guides/constraining-rotations/): keep only the yaw of a rotation or
  remove its roll, with a complete turret script.
- [Interpolation and easing](/guides/interpolation/): rescale between ranges, interpolate floats,
  vectors and rotations, and step values towards a target.

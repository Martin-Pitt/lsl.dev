Sets the vehicle float parameter param to value.

VEHICLE_ANGULAR_DEFLECTION_TIMESCALE33 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the vehicle to achieve full angular deflection
VEHICLE_ANGULAR_MOTOR_DECAY_TIMESCALE35 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the angular motor's effectiveness to decay toward zero
VEHICLE_ANGULAR_MOTOR_TIMESCALE34 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the vehicle to achive its full angular motor velocity
VEHICLE_BANKING_EFFICIENCY38 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} slider between -1 (leans out of turns), 0 (no banking), and +1 (leans into turns)
VEHICLE_BANKING_MIX39 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} slider between 0 (static banking) and 1 (dynamic banking)
VEHICLE_BANKING_TIMESCALE40 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the banking behavior to take full effect
VEHICLE_BUOYANCY27 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} slider between -1 (double-gravity) and 1 (full anti-gravity)

VEHICLE_HOVER_TIMESCALE26 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} period of time for the vehicle to achieve its hover height
VEHICLE_LINEAR_DEFLECTION_EFFICIENCY28 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} slider between 0 (no deflection) and 1 (maximum strength)
VEHICLE_LINEAR_DEFLECTION_TIMESCALE29 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the vehicle to redirect its velocity to be along its x-axis
VEHICLE_LINEAR_MOTOR_DECAY_TIMESCALE31 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the linear motor's effectiveness to decay toward zero
VEHICLE_LINEAR_MOTOR_TIMESCALE30 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the vehicleto achive its full linear motor velocity
VEHICLE_VERTICAL_ATTRACTION_EFFICIENCY36 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} slider between 0 (bouncy) and 1 (critically damped) attraction of vehicle z-axis to world z-axis (vertical)
VEHICLE_VERTICAL_ATTRACTION_TIMESCALE37 {{#if:{{#var:display_type}}|{{!}} [[float]]}} exponential timescale for the vehicle to align its z-axis to the world z-axis (vertical)
